**Title:** Services | Nikolaus Teply Restorations | Sydney  
**Meta:** We provide restoration, conservation, & repair services to your antiques or wooden furniture & restore it to its full glory. Enquire today!

**Images:** `public/images/new-slide2-opt.webp` (alt: antique restoration Sydney)

**H1:** Professional Antique Restoration and Conservation Services  
**H2:** Comprehensive Restoration Services

Restoration: structural repairs; veneer repairs/replacements; repair and replacement of related metal work; hand finishing (French Polish, oil, wax); re-laying baize and leathers; turning; re-caning; colour matching.

Conservation: original finishes and patina; antique lacquer / Chinoiserie and gilding; surface cleaning of non-wooden objects; internationally recognised guidelines.

Other: contemporary repairs; care advice; written + photographic documentation; complete refinishing; pickup and delivery; custom designed fine furniture; on-site work; upholstery.

Quote note: estimates from photos possible; in-person viewing preferred; detailed quote before work starts.
